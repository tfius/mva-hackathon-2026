**OptimusKG reachability — the NAD+ gap is not about graph size.** Ran §5.8's mechanism-anchored question against OptimusKG (one edge-table pass, no retraining). BUB1B has 96 protein interactors there against PrimeKG's 95 — nearly identical PPI neighbourhood despite 4x the total edges. Nicotinamide riboside, nicotinamide and niacin are all *nodes* in OptimusKG and **none is reachable**, because none has a drug-target edge into the SIRT2 neighbourhood. The only molecule with a SIRT2 edge in either graph is **cambinol, an inhibitor**.

**Why, and it generalises.** NR does not bind SIRT2 — it raises the NAD+ that SIRT2 consumes. Neither graph has an edge type for "increases availability of a cofactor this enzyme requires". **An intervention acting on a substrate pool rather than a protein target is invisible to a target-centric KG**, at any size. Schema limit, not data limit. Applies to every nutrient, cofactor and metabolic repurposing candidate.

**H3 gains a real route.** Tanespimycin reachable via **CEP57–HSP90AA1** — genuine mechanism, not a hub detour, since 17-AAG inhibits HSP90 and proteotoxic stress is the published basis. Quercetin same route. Chloroquine, acadesine, metformin still unreachable: their targets are autophagy/AMPK machinery, not checkpoint interactors.
