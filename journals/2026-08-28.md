Filtered: every autosome at 0.00071 ± 0.00006, max 2.6 σ. **No per-chromosome mosaicism above f ≈ 0.054.** Consistent with MVA, since variegated aneuploidy dilutes each chromosome below detection by construction.

**Correction, and it matters.** Allele B is *not* absent from gnomAD as first written — gnomAD v4 exomes carry 1 allele in 1,461,878 (AF 6.8e-07), confirmed against the gnomAD API; absent from genomes and dbSNP. Report and submission CSV both fixed. Also logged the predictor split: AlphaMissense 0.923 and MVP 0.852 vs REVEL 0.472.

**Exomiser applies BP1 to allele B and gets it wrong.** BP1 = "missense in a gene where truncating variants cause disease" — but biallelic truncating BUB1B is not viable, so MVA1 patients *require* a hypomorph in trans. The rule counts the disease's defining genotype as evidence against it. Drop BP1 → Likely pathogenic. Written up as a methods point.

**Track 2 mechanism drafted**, three literature-anchored hypotheses, all verified against primary sources: NAD+/SIRT2 stabilising BubR1 via K668 — and K668 survives on the missense allele, so this genotype is the one where it could work (North, EMBO J 2014); senolytics, whose foundational experiment was run in the BubR1 progeroid mouse and rescued muscle and adipose (Baker, Nature 2011); aneuploidy-selective AICAR/17-AAG (Tang & Amon, Cell 2011). Plus a contraindication list — MPS1/TTK, Aurora B, KIF11/PLK1 all weaken a checkpoint that is already the lesion.
